FODMAP

• Vegetables & Aromatics: Garlic, onions, shallots, leeks (bulb), asparagus, artichokes, mushrooms, cauliflower, and standard Brussels sprouts. • Fruits: Apples, pears, mangoes, peaches, cherries, watermelon, and dried fruits (like apricots, dates, and raisins). • Legumes & Beans: Chickpeas, lentils, kidney beans, black beans, baked beans, and soy beans. • Grains & Cereals: Wheat, rye, barley, and products made from them (such as standard bread, pasta, and cereals). • Dairy Products: Cow's, goat's, and sheep's milk, soft unripened cheeses (cottage, ricotta, cream cheese), yogurt, and ice cream. • Sweeteners & Polyols: Honey, high-fructose corn syrup, agave, sorbitol, mannitol, xylitol, and sugar-free confectionery.
